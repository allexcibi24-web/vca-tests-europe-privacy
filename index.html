VCA Tests Europe

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

VCA Tests Europe is an educational mobile application designed to help users prepare for VCA safety examinations and learn about workplace health and safety. This policy explains how information is handled in the app.

2. Information We Collect

The app allows users to enter a display name and may store the following information locally on their device:

  • User-selected display name
  • Language and appearance preferences
  • Learning progress and completed lessons
  • Quiz answers, scores and examination results
  • Course completion information for certificates

The app is designed to operate without requiring an online user account.

3. How Information Is Used

Locally stored information is used to personalise learning, remember preferences, save progress, display test results and generate course completion certificates.

4. Data Storage and Security

Learning progress and preferences are intended to be stored locally on the user's device. Clearing app storage, resetting progress or uninstalling the app may permanently delete locally stored information.

5. Data Sharing

The app does not intentionally transmit locally stored learning information to the developer or sell personal information to third parties.

This policy will be updated if future versions introduce additional data collection or third-party services.

6. Children's Privacy

VCA Tests Europe is primarily intended for people preparing for professional workplace safety examinations. It is not specifically designed for children.

7. Certificates

Certain courses may allow users to generate PDF completion certificates containing their chosen display name, course name and completion details. Users decide whether to share these certificates with others.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information may remain on the device until the user resets their progress, clears application data or uninstalls the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in app functionality, data handling or legal requirements. The latest version will be available on this page.
